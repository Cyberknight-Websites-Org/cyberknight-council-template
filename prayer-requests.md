## Submit a Prayer Request

Share the name of the person or intention you would like our council to remember in prayer.

Please provide either an email address or a phone number so we can reach you if needed. The reason for your request and your contact information will be kept private and used only for council follow-up. The recipient name may be included in the council newsletter while the request is current and the newsletter feature is enabled.
